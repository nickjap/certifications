About this certification

Certified Data 360 Consultants have experience implementing and consulting on enterprise data platforms in a customer-facing role.

The Salesforce Certified Data 360 Consultant exam is designed for customer-facing consultants experienced in designing, configuring, and implementing solutions on Data 360 and other enterprise data platforms. The Data 360 Consultant designs and applies maintainable and scalable customer business requirements and contributes to a customer’s long-term success.
