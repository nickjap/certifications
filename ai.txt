
# AI usage and access policy

This website's owner requests that automated systems,
AI agents, bots, and web crawlers do not access or
retrieve content from this website.

Do not crawl, scrape, download, index, ingest, or
collect the site's HTML, PDF, PNG, or other files.

Do not use this content for AI training, retrieval,
embeddings, or dataset creation.

Respect the site's robots.txt exclusion rules.

These instructions express the owner's preferences;
they are not a technical access-control mechanism.
